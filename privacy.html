Privacy Policy

Effective November 16, 2026

CameraShare lets one iPhone control the camera on another nearby iPhone. In short: we don't collect, see or sell any of your data.

What we collect

Nothing. CameraShare has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. It has no servers and doesn't use the internet.

How the two phones connect

Your two iPhones talk to each other directly, using Apple's MultipeerConnectivity over peer-to-peer Wi-Fi or Bluetooth. The connection is local to the phones near you, encrypted, and doesn't pass through any server, ours or anyone else's. The live preview, photos and videos only travel between your two phones.

Your photos and videos

  • Photos and videos are taken on the camera phone and kept at full resolution in CameraShare's Album, in the app's own storage on that phone.
  • A smaller copy of each photo is sent to the remote phone and kept in its Album. When a video finishes recording, a 720p copy is sent to the remote too. You can turn video copies off on CameraShare's home screen.
  • The Album stays on your phones. Nothing is uploaded to us or anyone else.
  • Like other app data, the Album is included in your phone's normal backups (iCloud Backup or your computer). Deleting CameraShare deletes its Album, so save anything you want to keep to Photos first.
  • Photos and videos go to your Photos library only when you choose Save to Photos, or if you turn on "Also save automatically to Photos". CameraShare only adds to Photos. It can't see or read the rest of your library.

Once saved, they're handled by the Photos app like any other photo or video, including iCloud Photos if you have it turned on.

Your name

The name you set on the home screen (by default, your iPhone's name) is shown to nearby iPhones running CameraShare, so you can pick the right camera. It's stored only on your phone.

Permissions

  • Camera: on the camera phone, to show the live view and take photos and videos.
  • Microphone: on the camera phone, to record sound with videos and Live Photos. It's only asked for when you switch to Video or turn on Live Photos. If you say no, videos are recorded without sound.
  • Local Network: to find and connect to your other iPhone nearby.
  • Photos (add only): to save the photos and videos you choose to your Photos library.

Children

CameraShare doesn't collect information from anyone, including children under 13.
