Privacy Policy

Effective November 16, 2026

CameraShare lets one iPhone control the camera on another nearby iPhone. In short: we don't collect, see or sell any of your data.

What we collect

Nothing. CameraShare has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. It has no servers and doesn't use the internet.

How the two phones connect

Your two iPhones talk to each other directly, using Apple's MultipeerConnectivity over peer-to-peer Wi-Fi or Bluetooth. The connection is local to the phones near you, encrypted, and doesn't pass through any server, ours or anyone else's. The live preview and the photos only travel between your two phones.

Your photos

  • Photos are taken on the camera phone and saved to that phone's Photos library.
  • A smaller copy is sent to the remote phone and saved to its Photos library.
  • You can turn either save off on CameraShare's home screen.
  • CameraShare only adds photos. It can't see or read the rest of your library.

Once saved, photos are handled by the Photos app like any other photo, including iCloud Photos if you have it turned on.

Your name

The name you set on the home screen (by default, your iPhone's name) is shown to nearby iPhones running CameraShare, so you can pick the right camera. It's stored only on your phone.

Permissions

  • Camera: on the camera phone, to show the live view and take photos.
  • Local Network: to find and connect to your other iPhone nearby.
  • Photos (add only): to save the photos you take.

Children

CameraShare doesn't collect information from anyone, including children under 13.
