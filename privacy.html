Privacy Policy

Effective November 16, 2026 · Last updated November 17, 2026

CameraShare is a camera app that works on one iPhone, and lets one iPhone control the camera on another nearby iPhone. In short: we don't collect, see or sell any of your data.

What we collect

Nothing. CameraShare has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. It has no servers and doesn't use the internet.

How the two phones connect

Your two iPhones talk to each other directly, using Apple's MultipeerConnectivity over peer-to-peer Wi-Fi or Bluetooth. The connection is local to the phones near you, encrypted, and doesn't pass through any server, ours or anyone else's. The live preview, photos and videos only travel between your two phones.

Your photos and videos

  • Photos and videos are taken on the camera phone and kept at full resolution in CameraShare's Album, in the app's own storage on that phone.
  • By default, the camera phone also saves each photo and video to your Photos library as soon as you take it. You can turn this off in CameraShare's Settings (the gear on the home screen) under "When this phone is the camera".
  • When a remote is connected, a smaller copy of each photo is sent to it and kept in its Album. When a video finishes recording, a 720p copy is sent to the remote too. You can turn video copies off in Settings.
  • The remote doesn't save to Photos automatically unless you turn that on in its Settings. You can save any item to Photos from either phone's Album.
  • The Album stays on your phones. Nothing is uploaded to us or anyone else.
  • Like other app data, the Album is included in your phone's normal backups (iCloud Backup or your computer). Deleting CameraShare deletes its Album, so make sure anything you want to keep is in Photos first.
  • CameraShare only adds to Photos. It can't see or read the rest of your library.

Once saved, they're handled by the Photos app like any other photo or video, including iCloud Photos if you have it turned on.

Assist

Assist is the optional framing help (hints, face and subject boxes, a level, grids, a histogram and camera readings). It is off until you turn it on.

  • Assist analyses the live preview on the phone you're using, with Apple's on-device image analysis. The analysis happens in memory while the preview is showing. Nothing from it is stored, and nothing is sent to us or anyone else.
  • When a remote is connected, the camera phone sends it the camera readings (such as ISO, shutter speed, white balance and frame rate), its battery level and heat warning, and the level reading, so the remote can show them. These only travel between your two phones, like the live preview.
  • Free storage and recording time left are shown only on the camera phone. They're never sent to the remote or anywhere else.

Your name

The name you set on the home screen (by default, your iPhone's name) is shown to nearby iPhones running CameraShare, so you can pick the right camera. It's stored only on your phone.

Permissions

  • Camera: on the camera phone, to show the live view and take photos and videos.
  • Microphone: on the camera phone, to record sound with videos and Live Photos. It's only asked for when you switch to Video or turn on Live Photos. If you say no, videos are recorded without sound.
  • Local Network: to find and connect to your other iPhone nearby.
  • Photos (add only): to save your photos and videos to your Photos library. The camera phone asks the first time you take a photo or video, because it saves to Photos automatically by default. The remote asks the first time you save to Photos. If you say no, captures are still kept in CameraShare's Album.
  • Motion sensor: on the camera phone, to show Assist's level. No permission is needed for this. The readings are used only for the level, only while it's needed, and aren't stored.

Children

CameraShare doesn't collect information from anyone, including children under 13.
