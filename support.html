Common questions

How do I get started?
You need two iPhones with CameraShare. On the phone that will take the picture, tap Camera. On the other, tap Remote and then tap the camera's name. Choose Photo, Portrait or Video on the remote, frame the shot and tap the shutter.

The remote doesn't find the camera.
Keep both phones close together, with Wi-Fi and Bluetooth turned on in Control Center (you don't need to be connected to a Wi-Fi network). Make sure Local Network access is allowed for CameraShare on both phones in Settings → Privacy & Security → Local Network. Keep the camera phone's screen open on the Camera view.

Do I need internet or Wi-Fi?
No. The phones connect to each other directly, so it works in a park or on a mountain. Wi-Fi and Bluetooth only need to be switched on.

Where are my photos? How do I save them to Photos?
Everything you take is kept in CameraShare's Album, on both phones. The camera phone has the full-resolution originals, and the remote has copies (smaller photos, and a 720p copy of each video). Open the Album from the home screen or the thumbnail next to the shutter, tap Select, choose your shots and tap Save to Photos. Live Photos and videos are saved as Live Photos and videos. You can also share or delete them there. To save everything to Photos as you go, turn on "Also save automatically to Photos" on the home screen. Copies on the remote are regular still photos, so save Live Photos and Portrait shots from the camera phone.

Will I lose my photos if I delete the app?
Yes. The Album is stored inside CameraShare, so deleting the app deletes it. Save anything you want to keep to Photos first. The Album is included in your normal iPhone backups.

Which lenses can I use?
Whatever the camera phone has: 0.5x, 1x, 2x or 3x on the back, plus the front camera. The remote only shows the lenses that phone supports.

Why does it ask for the microphone?
To record sound with videos and Live Photos. Only the camera phone asks, and only when you switch to Video or turn on Live Photos. If you say no, videos are recorded without sound.

Portrait isn't available.
Portrait needs depth from the camera phone: an iPhone with two or more rear cameras, or the front TrueDepth camera. If the camera phone has neither, Portrait doesn't appear as a mode on the remote. In Portrait, the remote only shows the lenses that support it, and switches lens for you if needed.

A video didn't arrive on the remote.
The remote gets its copy after recording stops, so long videos take a little while. Keep the phones connected and close together until it arrives. Check that "Send videos to the remote" is on, on the camera phone's home screen. If the phones disconnected, the video is still in the camera phone's Album at full quality.

I said no to camera, microphone or Photos access.
Tap Open Settings in the app, or go to Settings → CameraShare (Settings → Apps → CameraShare on iOS 18 and later), and turn the permission back on.

Does CameraShare cost anything?
No. It's free, with no ads, in-app purchases or accounts.
