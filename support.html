Common questions

How do I get started?
You need two iPhones with CameraShare. On the phone that will take the picture, tap Camera. On the other, tap Remote and then tap the camera's name. Frame the shot on the remote and tap the shutter.

The remote doesn't find the camera.
Keep both phones close together, with Wi-Fi and Bluetooth turned on in Control Center (you don't need to be connected to a Wi-Fi network). Make sure Local Network access is allowed for CameraShare on both phones in Settings → Privacy & Security → Local Network. Keep the camera phone's screen open on the Camera view.

Do I need internet or Wi-Fi?
No. The phones connect to each other directly, so it works in a park or on a mountain. Wi-Fi and Bluetooth only need to be switched on.

Where do my photos go?
The full-resolution photo is saved on the camera phone, and a copy is saved on the remote. You can turn either off under Photos on the home screen.

Which lenses can I use?
Whatever the camera phone has: 0.5x, 1x, 2x or 3x on the back, plus the front camera. The remote only shows the lenses that phone supports.

I said no to camera or Photos access.
Tap Open Settings in the app, or go to Settings → CameraShare (Settings → Apps → CameraShare on iOS 18 and later), and turn the permission back on.

Does CameraShare cost anything?
No. It's free, with no ads, in-app purchases or accounts.
