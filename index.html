Use a second iPhone as a big-screen camera remote.

Mount one iPhone on a tripod or selfie stick, or prop it up anywhere. Frame the shot on your other iPhone's full screen, then take the photo or video from there.

  • Live preview: see exactly what the camera phone sees, full screen on the remote, with controls laid out like the iPhone Camera app.
  • Photo, Portrait and Video: Live Photos if you want them, Portrait with depth so you can change the blur later in Photos, and 4K or 1080p video at 30 or 60 fps where the phone supports it.
  • Full control: shutter, pinch to zoom, lens (0.5x, 1x, 2x, 3x or front, depending on the phone), exposure, 3s or 10s self-timer, flash, tap-to-focus and a rule-of-thirds grid.
  • An Album on both phones: full-resolution originals on the camera phone and copies on the remote. Pick your favorites and save them to Photos, share them or delete them.
  • No internet needed: the phones connect directly and the connection is encrypted.
  • Private: no account, no ads, no tracking. Free.

Requires two iPhones with iOS 17 or later.
