Use a second iPhone as a big-screen camera remote.

Mount one iPhone on a tripod or selfie stick, or prop it up anywhere. Frame the shot on your other iPhone's full screen, then take the photo from there.

  • Live preview: see exactly what the camera phone sees, full screen on the remote.
  • Full control: shutter, lens (0.5x, 1x, 2x, 3x or front, depending on the phone), 3s or 10s self-timer, flash and tap-to-focus.
  • Photos on both phones: the full-resolution photo is saved on the camera phone and a copy on the remote. Either can be turned off.
  • No internet needed: the phones connect directly and the connection is encrypted.
  • Private: no account, no ads, no tracking. Free.

Requires two iPhones with iOS 17 or later.
