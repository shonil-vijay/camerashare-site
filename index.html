Use a second iPhone as a big-screen camera remote.

Mount one iPhone on a tripod or selfie stick, or prop it up anywhere. Frame the shot on your other iPhone's full screen, then take the photo or video from there. On its own, the camera phone is a full camera, with Assist to help you frame every shot.

  • Live preview: see exactly what the camera phone sees, full screen on the remote, with controls laid out like the iPhone Camera app.
  • Photo, Portrait and Video: Live Photos if you want them, Portrait with depth so you can change the blur later in Photos, and 4K or 1080p video at 30 or 60 fps where the phone supports it.
  • Full control: shutter, pinch to zoom, lens (0.5x, 1x, 2x, 3x or front, depending on the phone), exposure, 3s or 10s self-timer, flash and tap-to-focus.
  • Assist: optional, on-device help to frame the shot. Up to two hints at a time on placement and headroom, someone cut off at the edge, level, backlight and exposure, blinks and people looking away, and scene tips. Many apply with a tap.
  • Visual aids: face and subject boxes with optional subject focus, a level line, a live histogram, clipping zebras, and grids (rule of thirds, golden ratio, centre cross, diagonals, square).
  • Camera readings: ISO, shutter speed, EV, white balance, frame rate and AF/AE, plus the camera phone's battery and heat warning on the remote.
  • A full camera on one iPhone: every control works on the camera phone by itself, and a remote can join at any time.
  • Straight to Photos: the camera phone saves every shot to Photos automatically (you can turn this off), and keeps it in CameraShare's Album. The remote gets copies in its own Album to save, share or delete.
  • No internet needed: the phones connect directly and the connection is encrypted.
  • Private: no account, no ads, no tracking. Assist runs on your phones and nothing is sent to us. Free.

Requires iOS 17 or later. Use one iPhone as a camera, or two for the remote.
